# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Industry Performance Dataset

## Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an industry dataset containing 15,000 records and 12 variables. The goal is to identify trends, detect outliers, analyze relationships, and generate business insights.

## Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset Features
- Company Name
- Industry
- Country
- Region
- Employee Count
- Annual Revenue
- Profit Margin
- Customer Count
- Market Rating
- Founded Year
- Created Date

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Univariate Analysis
- Summary statistics
- Distribution analysis
- Skewness & Kurtosis

### 2. Outlier Detection
- Boxplots
- IQR Method
- Z-Score Method

### 3. Bivariate Analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Revenue vs Employee Count
- Revenue vs Customer Count

### 4. Multivariate Analysis
- Industry × Revenue × Employee Count
- Region × Market Rating × Profit Margin

### 5. Correlation Analysis
- Correlation Matrix
- Heatmap Visualization

## Key Insights
- Revenue distribution is positively skewed.
- Employee count strongly impacts revenue.
- Technology sector generates the highest revenue.
- Retail sector serves the largest customer base.
- Large enterprises contribute significantly to overall revenue.

## Conclusion
EDA revealed valuable business trends, industry patterns, and relationships that can support strategic decision-making and business intelligence initiatives.
